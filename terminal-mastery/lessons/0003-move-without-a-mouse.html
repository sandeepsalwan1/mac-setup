Lesson 03 · Neovim · 15 min

Move without a mouse

Vim is a language, not a shortcut list. Learn the grammar in one file and you can express any edit you can describe.

Know this before you start
The grammar[count] operator [count] motion-or-text-object
Sod2w = delete two words. ci" = change inside quotes.
Operatorsd delete · c change · y yank. Doubled = whole line.
Objectsi = inner (contents), a = a (contents + delimiters)

Do next: vimtutor, 25 minutes, offline. Then :help motion.txt and :help text-objects when you want the complete set. :help quickref is a one-page index of everything.

Do not use the arrow keys. They work, and they will keep your hands off the home row for a month. Two weeks of forcing hjkl makes it permanent.
