Panic card

Everything you need when you are stuck and cannot think. One page. Nothing here requires you to remember anything else.

Get out of Neovim

EscEsc :q! EnterQuit, lose changes. Always works.
:wq EnterSave and quit
:w EnterSave, stay
:q EnterQuit if nothing unsaved
ZZSave and quit, no colon
ZQQuit, lose changes, no colon
:qa! EnterQuit everything
Ctrl+cClose a plugin popup

Undo anything

uUndo
Ctrl+rRedo
UUndo all edits on this line
:earlier 5mRewind the file 5 minutes
:later 5mForward again

undofile is on, so undo survives quitting and reopening. You cannot lose work by saving.

Which mode am I in?

bottom-left blankNormal. Home. Keys are commands.
-- INSERT --Typing. Esc to leave.
-- VISUAL --Selecting. Esc to leave.
: at the bottomCommand line. Esc to cancel.

Esc always moves you toward Normal. Press it twice and you are home.

Traps unique to your config

Esc in NormalSaves the file. Not a no-op.
E32: No file nameYou pressed Esc in a nameless buffer. Harmless. :w name.txt fixes it.
mouse does nothingDeliberate, mouse = ''. Use Ctrl+d / Ctrl+u.
clipboard vanishedd / x are cuts. Use "_d to delete into the bin.
gd finds nothingNo LSP installed. Use * or Spaces.
