Daily review

Every question from all seven lessons, shuffled together and scheduled. Two minutes a day beats an hour of rereading.

Why this and not just rereading

Rereading builds fluency: it feels familiar, so you assume you know it. Fluency collapses the moment you are in a real terminal with a broken build. What you actually want is storage strength, and only three things build it:

RetrievalPulling the answer out of your head, not recognising it on a page. Every question here is recall.
SpacingAnswer right and the question comes back later, not sooner. The forgetting between attempts is what makes the next attempt work.
InterleavingHerdr, then Vim, then Nix, then Vim again. Mixed practice is harder and sticks far better than doing one topic in a block.

All three are deliberately uncomfortable. That is the signal it is working.

How the scheduling works

Leitner boxes. Get one right and it moves up a box; get it wrong and it drops straight back to box 1.

The bar at the top is your whole bank, coloured by box. Grey is "not seen yet". When it is mostly pale blue you are done, and most days will show nothing due at all.

The honest limit

Multiple choice tests recognition better than it tests production. It is the right tool for facts - which key, which flag, which mode - and the wrong tool for skill. For skill, use the motion trainer, which makes you produce real keystrokes, and the drills in each lesson, which make you produce real commands.

Do both. Two minutes here, five minutes there.
