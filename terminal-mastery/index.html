The five things most likely to bite you

Esc savesIn Neovim's normal mode, Escape runs :w. Not a no-op. On a nameless buffer it errors E32 - harmless.
mouse is offInside Neovim only. It still works in Herdr, where dragging auto-copies.
deletes cutd and x overwrite your macOS clipboard. "_dd deletes into the bin instead.
gd is deadNo LSP installed. Use * or Spaces.
preserve./rebuild.sh installs the shared baseline and leaves work-specific brews alone.
